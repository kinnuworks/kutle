# User testing

## Who

One person who works with batteries professionally (battery service, EV repair, recycling or electronics repair), tested remotely or in person for 15 minutes. Their name and employer stay private unless they agree otherwise; record only their role and years of experience.

## Protocol

Before starting, say: "We are testing the app, not you. Please think aloud." Do not explain the screens.

| # | Task | Success criteria | Record |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open passport #1 from its QR link and tell us whether this battery should be reused, repurposed or recycled, and why | Names the grade and points at the interval or the health history | Time, path taken, what they looked at first |
| 2 | Find out who last measured this battery's health and with which model | Finds the reading batch and the model entry | Time, any confusion |
| 3 | Check one reading on chain ("Verify a reading") | Runs the check and reads the result correctly | Did they understand what was proven? |
| 4 | In the attack console, run "claim more metal than the batteries held" and explain what happened | Explains that the books could not balance | Their own words |
| 5 | Look at the recycled-content section and say whether this battery would meet the 2031 rule for cobalt | Correct yes or no | Time |

After the tasks, ask:

1. What would you need to trust a used battery you were buying? Does this give it to you?
2. What is missing or wrong compared with how you assess packs today?
3. Who in your work would use this, and who would resist it?
4. On a scale of 1 to 5, how likely would you be to use a passport like this when buying a used pack?
